## Project 1

### Which tools did you use?

<!-- e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Midjourney, an image/sound generator, … -->

I used ChatGPT.

### What did you use them for?

<!-- Where did AI help? Ideas, writing/debugging code, creating visual or audio assets, explaining a concept, refactoring, … -->

Chat GPT helped me understand and combine programming concepts. I selected specific topics from class such as loops, arrays, modulo, coordinate system, dist(), map() and textToPoints() and asked how I could incorporate them in the code to execute my idea. Further, it helped me troubleshoot the code and quickly detect errors. I also created a CSV.file with Chat GPT. 

### How did you use them?

<!-- Include the key prompts you used, or a short summary of the back-and-forth. Be specific enough that someone could follow your process. -->

After sharing my inputs from class (as mentioned above), I asked for a sample code. I reviewed it to identify unfamiliar functions and unnecessary complexity.

First, I tried to modify and simplify the code myself and later asked Chat GPT to further simplify the code, referencing the techniques we had learnt in class. 

I also used ChatGPT to explain individual lines and functions so I could understand how they contributed to the overall effect.

### What was your own contribution?

<!-- What did YOU decide, change, reject, or build on top of the AI's output? Which creative and technical choices were yours? -->

I searched for visual inspiration online. Both the design and interaction were my own ideas. Based on my inspiration board, I determined how the final project should look and which visual effects I wanted to achieve. 

At first, I repeated the sketches from our class to noted down, which specific effects I wanted to use. 

Further, I actively evaluated, rejected and modified the code given by Chat GPT. One goal was to avoid overly complicated code, so I could actually understand what I was programming. 

However, I decided to keep unfamiliar concepts such as alphaVal, if they resulted in a better solution. I made sure to understand these concepts and added comments explaining their effects.

### Reflection

<!-- One or two sentences: what worked well, what didn't, and what you learned about using AI as a creative tool. -->

My priority was to enjoy the process and to keep it simple. Especially the first assignment was easy to follow. 

I found AI really helpful to understand the code, bring separate concepts together, keeping an overview of everything as well as working more efficiently.

In the future, I will still rely on AI to complete such complex projects. 

Nonetheless, I would like to become more independent, starting with simpler sketches and develop a better understanding for the programming language itself.  

I am statisfied with the fact that I didn't use AI for my creative vision. I had a clear visual idea early on and used AI primarily as a technical and educational tool to help realize it.
