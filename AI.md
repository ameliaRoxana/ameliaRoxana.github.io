### What was your own contribution?

<!-- What did YOU decide, change, reject, or build on top of the AI's output? Which creative and technical choices were yours? -->

I searched for visual inspiration online and repeated the sketches from our class to note down, which specific effects I wanted to use. For the first project it were mainly the inputs for the loops, arrays, modulo, coordinate system, use of dist() and textToPoint(). After giving the input and references from class to chat GPT, I scanned the code, to see where I could simplfy it. I tried to write it in a way, we learnt in class by myself and if that didn't work I asked Chat GPT how I could write it in the desired way. So, I rejected the first best output and thouroughly anaylsed and modified it before handing in the final version. For example, AI had the tendency to use things I didn't know yet. If the code was unfamiliar to me, I figured out what kind of effect it has and wrote a comment, so I understood why I kept it and what effect it had. 
Due to my inspiration board, I decided what the final outcome should look like and what meaning the interaction or visual style had. Further, Chat GPT helped me understand the code more and repeat learnt knowledge again.

### Reflection

<!-- One or two sentences: what worked well, what didn't, and what you learned about using AI as a creative tool. -->

My priority was to enjoy the process and keep it simple. Especially the first assignment was easy to understand and had a gradual process. I found AI to be quite helpful to understand the code and bring seperate elements together. I did struggle with keeping an overview of everything, especially as a beginner and the AI helped me to work more efficiantly. In the future, I would still need AI to complete such projects, but would like to learn how to do simple codes by myself that maybe don't rely on so many einzeln things. I am also statisfied, that I didn't use AI for my creative vision. I knew early on, how I wanted things to look. Due to the efficiany I could go thorugh different variations quite quickly an dcompare what worked or not.
